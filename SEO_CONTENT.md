## What Is MRI Safety Checker, Body Jewelry?

The MRI Safety Checker for Body Jewelry is a free web tool that classifies 17 common body jewelry materials according to ASTM F2503, the standard practice for marking medical devices for safety in the magnetic resonance environment. The tool accepts material names, alloy grades, and common aliases as input, then returns one of four classifications: MR Safe, MR Conditional, MR Unsafe, or Verify Grade.

The tool's dataset includes polymers (BioFlex®, PTFE, nylon/acrylic), implant-grade metals (titanium ASTM F136, niobium, 316LVM steel, gold, platinum), and unsafe materials (ferromagnetic steel, carbon steel, silver, nickel silver, magnetic jewelry). Each entry includes field strength limits, SAR limits, clinical notes, common uses, and manufacturer information where applicable.

Users can either search for a specific material using the search input or browse all materials grouped by safety classification. Results display in an expandable card format showing the ASTM classification, clinical guidance, technical specifications, and product links for Poli International materials.

## Who Should Use This Tool

**Tattoo and Piercing Studio Professionals:** Studio artists and piercers can use this tool to advise clients on appropriate jewelry for upcoming MRI scans. The tool helps identify which materials require removal and which can remain in place.

**MRI Technicians and Radiographers:** Medical imaging professionals can quickly verify whether a patient's body jewelry is safe to scan, reducing delays and ensuring patient safety.

**Patients with Body Piercings:** Individuals scheduled for an MRI can check their jewelry materials before their appointment, saving time and avoiding last-minute removal.

**Healthcare Facility Safety Officers:** Staff responsible for MRI safety protocols can reference the tool for standardized material classifications and clinical notes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What does ASTM F2503 mean for body jewelry?
ASTM F2503 is the standard practice for marking medical devices and other items for safety in the magnetic resonance environment. The MRI Safety Checker classifies body jewelry materials according to this standard into MR Safe, MR Conditional, or MR Unsafe categories.

### 2. Is titanium jewelry safe for MRI?
Implant-grade titanium (ASTM F136, Ti-6Al-4V ELI) is classified as MR Conditional up to 3T with SAR limits of 4 W/kg. Most radiographers accept titanium jewelry, though some facilities require removal for head or neck scans due to potential susceptibility artifact.

### 3. Can I wear BioFlex jewelry during an MRI?
Yes. BioFlex is classified as MR Safe with no known hazards in any MR environment. It is non-ferromagnetic, non-conductive, and generates no RF heating or MRI artifact. Patients can proceed to scan without removal, though you should confirm with the attending radiographer for site protocol.

### 4. Why is sterling silver listed as MR Unsafe?
Silver is diamagnetic with very low susceptibility, meaning no significant magnetic force. However, it can generate RF heating and significant susceptibility artifact in MRI images. Most MRI facilities require removal as a precaution, and silver is not certified for MRI use.

### 5. What does "Verify Grade" mean?
The "Verify Grade" classification applies to materials like "surgical steel" when the specific grade is unknown. Without a material certificate, safety cannot be confirmed. The tool recommends replacing with a BioFlex retainer or verified titanium if the grade is unknown.

### 6. How many materials does the tool cover?
The tool covers 17 body jewelry materials, including polymers (BioFlex, PTFE, nylon/acrylic), implant-grade metals (titanium, niobium, 316LVM steel, gold, platinum, anodised titanium), and unsafe materials (ferromagnetic steel, carbon steel, silver, nickel silver, magnetic jewelry).

### 7. What field strength limits are listed for MR Conditional materials?
Field strength limits vary by material: implant-grade titanium and niobium are conditional up to 3T, while 316LVM stainless steel is conditional up to 1.5T. Gold and platinum are conditional up to 3T. Always confirm with your facility's specific MRI scanner.

### 8. Can I browse all materials at once?
Yes. Click the "Browse all materials" tab to view all 17 materials grouped by safety classification (MR Safe, MR Conditional, MR Unsafe, Verify Grade). Each group displays the number of materials and expandable cards with full details.

### 9. Does the tool replace clinical judgement?
No. The tool includes a clinical disclaimer stating it is for informational purposes only and does not replace clinical judgement or site-specific MRI safety protocols. Always confirm jewelry material and MR condition with the attending radiographer or MRI safety officer.

### 10. What if my material isn't in the dataset?
If a material is not found, the tool displays a "not found" card advising that unverified materials should be treated as unverified and the radiographer should be consulted. The tool suggests considering a BioFlex retainer for guaranteed MR Safe status.
